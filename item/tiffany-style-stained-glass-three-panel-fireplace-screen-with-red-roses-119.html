About the Item

A three-panel folding fireplace screen in the Tiffany style, composed of hundreds of hand-cut stained-glass pieces copper-foiled and soldered in the manner of Louis Comfort Tiffany. Deep red roses with green and yellow foliage climb a blue-and-white lattice ground. Placed before a hearth or a window, it throws jewel-toned light across the room. Design closely matches the Chloe 'Canna' Tiffany-style 3-panel fireplace screen (520+ glass pieces). Likely late-20th-century production in the Tiffany manner, not period Tiffany Studios.
