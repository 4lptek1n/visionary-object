Tiffany-Style Green Willow Stained Glass Table Lamp

About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp with a flared conical shade of mottled emerald-green glass worked in a herringbone willow-leaf pattern that glows a rich yellow-green when lit. The shade stands on a patinated metal base with a twisted fluted column, a domed foot modelled with spreading root relief, and twin pull chains with ball ends. A decorative late-20th-century reproduction in the Art Nouveau taste. Tape-measure photos are present but not anchored to the lamp (one shows the tape held above the shade, another at the shade rim with no fixed start), so no measurement can be read; dimensions recorded as not legible. No maker's mark visible; attribution is stylistic only.
