About the Item

A white modernist villa crowns a moonlit cliff above a glittering sea, a curved swimming pool mirroring the night sky — a dreamscape in the unmistakable manner of American fantasy-landscape artist Jim Buckels. Finely detailed, richly colored and presented in an ornate gilt frame, this is a statement piece for collectors of contemporary romantic fantasy art. Approximately 48 inches (121.9 cm) tall framed. Composition, subject and style are a textbook match to Jim Buckels (b. 1948, American fantasy landscape artist; cf. his 'Druid Point' and other moonlit villa works). Signature present lower right but illegible in photos — attribution kept at 'Unknown' per house rule. Height 48 in (121.9 cm) framed; width illegible.
