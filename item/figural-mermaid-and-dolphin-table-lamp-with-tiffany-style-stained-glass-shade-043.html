About the Item

A figural table lamp in the Tiffany Revival taste: a bronze-finished mermaid rides a leaping dolphin rising from stylised waves, the dolphin's belly formed of a mosaic of leaded art glass that glows as a night light. Above, an amber mosaic umbrella shade shelters three sockets with pull chains. The underside of the base is inscribed with the edition number 124/200 and the name Judy, and a UL label dates the lamp to December 1999. Base underside inscribed '124/200 Judy' (limited edition of 200); UL label reads '12/99 E185743 PORTABLE LAMP ISSUE NO. BH-87,620', dating manufacture to December 1999. Double illumination: 3-socket shade light plus stained-glass dolphin-belly night light. No tape-measure photos in folder; dimensions not legible.
