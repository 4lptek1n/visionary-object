About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp crowned with a domed leaded-glass shade ablaze with crimson blossoms and green foliage, its scalloped rim glowing when lit. The shade rests on a patinated bronze stem with bark-like texture rising from an organic root-form base. Twin pull chains with ball ends complete the romantic silhouette. A decorative revival piece in the Art Nouveau taste. Cover shows lamp lit; ring finial, two pull chains with ball terminals; bark-textured stem; root-form base. No maker's mark visible.
