About the Item

A Tiffany-style Mission table lamp in the 'Dark Star' pattern: a wide conical leaded shade of glowing caramel-amber glass rayed with bold dark geometric starbursts and iridescent green-violet accents. The shade is carried on a dark patinated metal base with a tall, tapered triple-column stem and a square stepped foot, fitted with twin pull chains. A decorative late-20th-century reproduction in the Arts and Crafts / Prairie taste. No tape-measure photos in folder; dimensions not legible. No maker's mark visible; attribution is stylistic only ('in the manner of Tiffany Studios').
