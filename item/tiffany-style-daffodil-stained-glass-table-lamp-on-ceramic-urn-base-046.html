About the Item

A Tiffany-style daffodil table lamp with a bell-shaped leaded shade of golden-orange daffodils amid green foliage and pale blue glass, finished with a white-and-green tile border. The shade is paired with a glossy reddish-brown glazed ceramic urn-form base with handles on a square stepped foot. A decorative 20th-century reproduction in the Art Nouveau taste; the ceramic-and-glass pairing suggests later decorative manufacture rather than period Tiffany Studios work. One tape-measure photo shows the finial at ~26 in, but the tape is not anchored at the lamp's foot (foot out of frame), so total height cannot be determined; dimensions recorded as not legible. No maker's mark visible; attribution is stylistic only.
