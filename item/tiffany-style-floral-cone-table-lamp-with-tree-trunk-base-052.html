About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp with a conical leaded-glass shade dense with stylised blossoms in crimson, rose pink, cobalt and aqua against mottled green foliage, finished with a flame-form glass finial. The coppery tree-trunk textured standard rises to a round stepped base. Pull chains and the vivid botanical shade make it a striking centrepiece for a living room or conservatory. Shade height 8.5 in from two tape photos (finial top at 22 / shade bottom at 13.5; tape zero at shade bottom edge). Shade width not legible (tape zero end out of frame). No maker's marks visible; modern reproduction in the manner of Tiffany Studios.
