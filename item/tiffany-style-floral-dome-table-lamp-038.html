About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp with a domed leaded-glass shade of pink tulips and golden and azure blossoms on a mottled mauve ground. The short patinated-bronze stem with leaf relief stands on a floral-embossed base, with twin pull chains below the shade. A tape-measure photo indicates a height of about 11 inches. A decorative revival piece in the Art Nouveau taste. Dimensions: Height approx. 28 cm (11 in) — read from tape-measure photo (11_orijinal.jpg); shade diameter not measured Multicolor floral dome (pink/red tulips, yellow and blue blossoms, mauve ground); leaf-relief stem; floral-embossed base; two pull chains. Height read from tape photo 11_orijinal.jpg as difference between finial-top mark (~22 in) and table-level mark (~11 in). No maker's mark visible.
