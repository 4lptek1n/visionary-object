About the Item

A Tiffany-style Laburnum table lamp after Tiffany Studios' celebrated early-1900s design: a domical shade cascading with yellow laburnum blossoms among green and rose foliage over mottled amber glass. The mahogany-bronze base is dressed with stylised shell and lotus-leaf details and twin pull chains. A luminous, highly decorative piece for a hallway or living room. Laburnum pattern in the manner of Tiffany Studios' circa 1900 design. No tape-measure photos in folder; dimensions not legible. No maker's marks visible.
