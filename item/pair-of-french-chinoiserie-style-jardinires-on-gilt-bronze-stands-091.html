About the Item

A pair of French chinoiserie-style jardinières, each with a flared trumpet-form body in red lacquer hand-painted with butterflies and insects, mounted with gilt-bronze handles and raised on scrolled bronze tripod legs. A dramatic orientalist statement in the 19th-century French taste. Dimensions: 12 in W x 23 in H each (30 x 58 cm) — read from tape measure; sold as a pair No maker marks visible. Described as French taste rather than French manufacture.
