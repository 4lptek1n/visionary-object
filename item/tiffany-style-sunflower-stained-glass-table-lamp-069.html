About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp with a domed stained-glass shade showing yellow sunflowers, blue and pink blossoms and green foliage against a dark navy lattice ground. It stands on a scalloped trumpet-form metal base in an antique-bronze finish, fitted with a pull chain. No chips, cracks or shade damage were observed in the photographs. Dimensions: Overall height approx. 24 in (61 cm); shade diameter approx. 17 in (43 cm) Modern Tiffany-style reproduction; no maker's mark visible in the photographs. The scalloped trumpet base design is shared with the lamp in ilan_068, which carries a different (floral-lattice) shade.
