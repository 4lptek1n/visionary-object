About the Item

Titled 'Shallow Water' and inscribed 'R. Gates, 2007' on the verso, this minimalist abstract divides the canvas between a pale, line-scored sky and a teal grid of dark dots suggesting water. A quiet, meditative work in the spirit of Agnes Martin, suited to serene contemporary spaces. Artist name, date '2007' and title 'Shallow Water' handwritten on the verso; no signature on the front. Artist not independently verifiable — attribution based solely on the verso inscription.
