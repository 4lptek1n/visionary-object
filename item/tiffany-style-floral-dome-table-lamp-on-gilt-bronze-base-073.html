About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp whose broad dome shade blooms with creamy daisies, magenta and amethyst blossoms against an opalescent ground, finished with a green glass mosaic border. It stands on an ornate gilt-bronze column base with mask and foliate casting, raised on scrolled wrought-iron tripod legs. A decorative 20th-century reproduction in the manner of Tiffany Studios. Shade height read from vertical tape photo (10_orijinal): shade lower rim at approx. 2 in, finial top at approx. 14 in. Diameter tape photo (11_orijinal) shows only one rim edge and is not readable for full width. Total lamp height not legible.
