Tiffany-Style Twelve-Light Lily Table Lamp

Price Upon Request

About the Item

A twelve-light lily table lamp in the manner of Tiffany Studios: a dozen hand-blown glass lily shades in amber-gold with green mottling nod on curving patinated-metal stems. The stems rise from a bronze-toned base modelled as a cluster of lily pads with buds and frogs. A decorative late-20th-century reproduction of one of Tiffany's most celebrated lamp forms, prized for its pond-garden romance. Tape-measure photos are present but not anchored (vertical tape not set at the lamp's foot; horizontal tape unanchored), so no measurement can be read; dimensions recorded as not legible. No maker's mark visible; attribution is stylistic only.
