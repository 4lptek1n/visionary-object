About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp with a broad scalloped shade of autumn leaves in fiery red, orange and gold, overlaid with a verdigris pierced-metal crown. The twisted patinated-bronze stem and leaf-relief base support twin pull chains with ball ends. A tape-measure photo indicates a height of about 16.5 inches. A decorative revival piece in the Art Nouveau taste.
