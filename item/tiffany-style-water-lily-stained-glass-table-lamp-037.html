About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp whose conical leaded-glass shade portrays white and golden water lilies floating among lily pads on blue water. The sinuous patinated-bronze stem, with whiplash Art Nouveau curves, rises from an organic molded base. A decorative revival piece capturing the pond-lily motif beloved of the Tiffany Studios. Water/pond lily motif; whiplash-curved stem; organic base; small flame finial. No maker's mark visible. No tape-measure photo among originals (02-14).
