About the Item

A two-part memorabilia set: a black ring binder holding a reproduction of the GoodFellas screenplay whose white title page carries the film's credits and a dense scatter of blue and black cast signatures, shown beside a black lacquered display plaque. The plaque mounts the familiar red-and-sepia GoodFellas one-sheet image of the three leads above a small painted clapperboard and a gilt nameplate. A numbered certificate of authenticity on clapperboard letterhead accompanies the set. Medium: printed paper script in a binder with reproduction signatures, plus a laminated poster print on a lacquered wood plaque. Signed across the script title page, reading "Mike Starr; Julie Garfield; Samuel L. Jackson; Tony Darrow; Michael Imperioli". Edition: 270/500, limited edition of 500 per display, reproduction signatures. Accompanied by a certificate: Certificate of Authenticity for the Limited Edition Autographed Script Series, hand-numbered 270/500, stating plainly that the script is a faithful reproduction carrying reproduction signatures. Inscribed on the reverse or mount: GoodFellas; Written by Nicholas Pileggi & Martin Scorsese; Directed by Martin Scorsese; Produced by Irwin Winkler. Condition: Clean and complete; a retail tag hangs from the display, tape residue on the plaque below the poster, minor scuffing to the black lacquer.
