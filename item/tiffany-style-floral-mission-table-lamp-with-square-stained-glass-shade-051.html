Tiffany-Style Floral Mission Table Lamp with Square Stained Glass Shade

· 20th century

Price Upon Request

About the Item

A Tiffany-style Mission table lamp with a square stained-glass shade of floral panels in ruby red, cobalt blue, amber and green, studded with glass cabochons and framed by scrolled metal corner brackets. The shade is carried on a dark bronze fluted column over a domed footed base with embossed foliage. Three pull chains control each socket separately, casting a jewel-toned glow ideal for a study or library corner. Tape photos: finial top at 23.5 in, table surface at approx. 4 in; shade height 8.5 in (tape zero at shade bottom edge). Shade width not legible (tape zero end out of frame). Same model as ilan_050. No maker's marks visible; modern reproduction in the manner of Tiffany Studios.
