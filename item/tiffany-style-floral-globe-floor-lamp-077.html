About the Item

A Tiffany-style floor lamp crowned by a spherical stained-glass globe shade, its floral mosaic in amber, blue and violet accented with jewel cabochons, glowing warmly when lit. A slender patinated-bronze pole with beaded pull chains rises from a weighted round base with a beaded rim. A decorative 20th-century reproduction, likely sold as a matching set with the dome-shaded floor lamp in ilan_076. Shade height read from vertical tape photo (10_orijinal): shade lower rim at approx. 43.5 in, shade crown at approx. 54.5 in. Shade diameter and total lamp height not legible. Pole and base design are identical to ilan_076, suggesting the same workshop or a matched pair; the two shades differ (globe vs. dome) and they are kept as separate listings.
