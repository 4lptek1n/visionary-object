About the Item

A long horizontal abstraction built from broad black gestures dragged across warm ochre, bone and pale grey. A scratched grid shows through at the lower left and a thin red note flickers near the top. The surface is worked with palette knife and brush and reads as weathered plaster. Signed in black at the lower left. Broad silver-gilt frame. Medium: acrylic and mixed media on canvas. Signed lower left. Condition: no visible damage.
