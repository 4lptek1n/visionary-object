About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp with a domed stained-glass shade patterned in rust-red and amber daisy blossoms on a cream ground with blue and green foliage. It stands on a fluted dark-metal base with original pull chains and pierced finial cap. No chips, cracks or shade damage were observed in the photographs. Dimensions: Overall height approx. 18.5 in (47 cm); shade diameter approx. 16.5 in (42 cm) Modern Tiffany-style reproduction; no maker's mark visible in the photographs. Shade and base are original to the lamp.
