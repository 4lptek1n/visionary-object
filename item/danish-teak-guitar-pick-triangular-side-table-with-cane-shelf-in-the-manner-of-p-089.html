About the Item

A 1960s Danish triangular 'guitar pick' side table in teak with a woven-cane lower magazine shelf, in the manner of Poul Jensen for Selig. The lipped triangular top has a beautifully figured grain; the cane shelf is intact. Handwriting is visible on the underside frame. An iconic piece of Danish modern design. Dimensions: 27 in W x 22 in H (69 x 56 cm) — read from tape measure Form matches the documented Poul Jensen for Selig guitar-pick table exactly (27 in wide is the catalog size); no Selig label legible in photos, so kept as 'in the manner of'.
