About the Item

A sculptural black metal table lamp in the Postmodern taste of the 1980s, its flared triple-post base crowned by an open circle motif in the spirit of the Memphis Group. The wide conical metal shade, finished in matte black, is topped with a sphere finial. Bold geometry and a monochrome palette make it a graphic statement piece. Shade appears original to base. Measurement photos (tape measure) are ambiguous and unreadable; dimensions recorded as not legible.
