About the Item

A large Tiffany-style table lamp crowned with a domed stained-glass shade in a peacock-feather motif — cobalt and turquoise glass feather 'eyes' ringed by cream, mauve and amber scallops. It is carried on a figural dark-bronze base cast in relief with peacock plumage, fitted with original pull chains. No chips, cracks or shade damage were observed in the photographs. Dimensions: Overall height approx. 28 in (71 cm); shade diameter approx. 18 in (46 cm) — tape measure partially legible in photos Modern Tiffany-style reproduction; no maker's mark visible in the photographs. Shade and base are original to the lamp.
