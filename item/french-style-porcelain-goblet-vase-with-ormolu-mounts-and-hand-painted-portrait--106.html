About the Item

A tall Continental porcelain goblet-form vase with gilt-metal mounts, in the French Neoclassical taste. The white body is reserved with a hand-painted cartouche of a lady in 18th-century dress, framed by gilt scrollwork, with a pierced gallery rim, figural mask handles and a shaped footed base. Dimensions: Not available — no measurement photos in set Unmarked (no base/mark photo in set). Ormolu-style mounts: pierced rim gallery, mask-and-tassel handles, molded foot. Portrait cartouche of a lady in blue 18th-c. dress. Late 19th/early 20th century French taste.
