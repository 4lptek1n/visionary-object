About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp with a conical leaded-glass shade depicting creamy calla lilies amid emerald leaves on a textured clear ground, finished with a beaded jewel border at the rim. It stands on a twisted patinated-bronze stem above a scrolled, richly ornamented base. The small dark finial and jewel trim add a jewel-like finish. A decorative revival piece in the Art Nouveau taste. Calla lily motif; beaded jewel border at shade rim; twisted stem; scrolled ornate base. No maker's mark visible.
