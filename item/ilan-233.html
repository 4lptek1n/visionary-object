About the Item

A brisk botanical abstraction in olive, acid green and warm ochre. Dark verticals of bamboo cut the sheet while paler stems and small white blossoms scatter across a sand-coloured wash. The handling is fast and confident, with drybrush scratches over flat washes. Signed along the lower right. Set in a slim gilt frame with a deep olive mat. Medium: watercolour and gouache on paper. Signed lower right of the sheet. Inscribed on the reverse or mount: cursive signature at the lower right, not legible at this resolution. Condition: no visible damage.
