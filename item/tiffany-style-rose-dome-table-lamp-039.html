About the Item

A Tiffany-style table lamp with a domed leaded-glass shade of blush roses and green foliage on a warm amber ground, its rim softly scalloped. The reeded patinated-bronze stem rises from a ribbed dome base, with twin pull chains ending in ball drops. Please note a curved crack to one amber panel visible in detail photography. A decorative revival piece in the Art Nouveau taste. Blush-rose dome on amber ground; reeded stem; ribbed dome base; two pull chains. Curved crack visible in one amber glass panel near the shade top (11_orijinal.jpg). No maker's mark visible. No tape-measure photo among originals (02-14).
