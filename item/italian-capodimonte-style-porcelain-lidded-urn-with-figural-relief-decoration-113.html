About the Item

An Italian lidded porcelain urn in the Capodimonte taste, the ovoid body modelled in high relief with classical figures amid scrolling drapery and foliage, painted in soft enamels with gilt highlights. Photographed with the domed cover removed and placed in front; foot detail shows painted floral garlands.
